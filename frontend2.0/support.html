Frequently Asked Questions

How are points calculated?
Points are awarded based on the predefined task values and are automatically added to your profile upon verification.
Why was my submission rejected?
Submissions may be rejected if the image appears AI-generated or does not match the selected task category.
Can I re-upload a task?
Yes, you can re-upload a task with valid proof if your previous submission was rejected.
What kind of images are allowed?
Only JPG and JPEG image formats are accepted.
